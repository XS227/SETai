      The prize digit root is a chosen rule

      Example: EUR 120,000,000 gives 1+2 = 3. Changing currency may change the digit root. This is a symbolic link to the amount, not a physical property of the draw. Currency and rounding must be fixed in advance.

      Historical Euro numbers are mapped to the pool of 8, 10 or 12 that applied at the time. Out-of-range values wrap into the pool; duplicates receive deterministic replacements. The random expectation uses the same historical rules.
