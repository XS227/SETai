Questions behind the numbers

What are the latest EuroJackpot winning numbers?

The latest recorded result is shown above, with its draw date. Open the results archive to see every available draw since 23 March 2012. Numbers are sourced from WestLotto, an official lottery operator.

Can AI predict the EuroJackpot winning numbers?

AI can analyse past draws and generate testable selections. This lab has not established a reliable predictive advantage. In a fair independent draw, past outcomes do not determine the next winning line.

How many numbers do you choose in EuroJackpot?

A line contains five distinct main numbers from 1 to 50 and two distinct Euro numbers from 1 to 12. Draws take place on Tuesdays and Fridays.

Can the same EuroJackpot numbers be drawn again?

Yes. A previously drawn combination remains eligible. Our archive analysis compares complete lines, sets of five main numbers and Euro-number pairs separately. The history filter changes the suggested selection pool, not the lottery's odds.

What is a numerology lottery line?

A symbolic selection based on a birthday, personal year, month and day, life path and optional name numbers. The date-and-prize experiment can add a known announced prize amount. These rules do not demonstrate increased lottery odds.

Can I buy a EuroJackpot ticket here?

No. SETAEI is an independent research and number-selection tool. Copy your chosen lines and use a licensed lottery operator where you are eligible to play.
