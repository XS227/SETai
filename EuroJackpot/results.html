How to read EuroJackpot results

Main numbers come from 1–50. The Euro-number pool was 1–8 before 10 October 2014, 1–10 until March 2022, and 1–12 from 25 March 2022. The archive preserves the rules that applied to each draw.

Drawn numbers are recorded whether or not any player matched the jackpot. Use the repetition analysis to compare complete combinations, main-number sets and Euro-number pairs.
