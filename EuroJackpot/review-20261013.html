The AI method, in plain English

  1. Look backwards. For each number, count appearances in the last 50 draws, its frequency over the eligible history, and how many draws have passed since its last appearance.
  2. Learn small adjustments. A regularised logistic model combines those features. It starts near equal weights and limits adjustments to avoid treating noisy patterns as certainty. Euro-number history resets when the number pool changes.
  3. Make a weighted random selection. Select five different main numbers and two different Euro numbers without replacement. More weight means more chance of being selected by this generator, not a proven greater chance in the actual lottery.
  4. Make it repeatable. A fixed seed uses the model version, target date, last available result date, mode and line index. AI selections 1 and 2 use the same model with different line indices.
  5. Freeze the line before the draw. Keep the original values and later compare them with the official result. Friday's existing forecasts remain unchanged.

AI selection 2 is not the seven highest-scoring numbers. Euro number 8 had a lower model score than the equal-weight baseline and still entered through the weighted random selection. The method reproduced the original line using only the 997 earlier draws; today's result was excluded.

What this result tells us

One fixed Euro pair has a 1 in 66 chance (about 1.52%) of matching both Euro numbers in a fair draw. Across the two registered AI pairs for this draw, the chance of at least one pair matching was 2 in 66 (about 3.03%): both pairs are distinct. The exact 1+2 category has a chance of about 1 in 188 per line.

Both AI lines must count in the evaluation. Their main-number matches were 0 and 1; their Euro-number matches were 1 and 2. One successful line does not establish improved future odds, and none matched 5+2. Keep the method fixed and track every future result against random controls.

The earlier calendar result of 13.4% more historical individual-number matches belongs to a different method. The calendar line scored 0+0 here; it cannot be credited with the AI result.
