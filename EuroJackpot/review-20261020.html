What did the model select?

The model used history through 2026-10-13, as recorded when these lines were registered. Frequency and absence features gave small adjustments around equal weights. Weighted random sampling then selected the lines; it did not reliably identify the winning set.

The actual winning numbers had these model weights before the draw. Ranking them after the result is an explanation, not information we could use to know the answer in advance.
