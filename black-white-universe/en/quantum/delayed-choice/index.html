IScientific description

In this experiment, photons are split into two possible paths.

Whether we observe or erase their path information

determines if they behave like particles or waves.

Astonishingly, even when the decision to erase is made after the photons have been detected,

the interference pattern adjusts as if the future choice affected the past.

Reference: PNAS (2013), Ma et al., “Quantum erasure with causally disconnected choice” · pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1213201110
