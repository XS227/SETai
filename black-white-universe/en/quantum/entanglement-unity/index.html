IScientific background

Quantum entanglement describes a phenomenon where two or more particles remain connected, even when separated by vast distances — a change in one instantly affects the other.

This non-local behavior, verified by numerous experiments (Aspect, Zeilinger, and Clauser — Nobel Prize in Physics, 2022), challenges the classical idea that information can only travel at the speed of light.

Recent experiments — like those performed at Delft University (2015) and Vienna (2017) — have confirmed “loophole-free” entanglement, showing correlations that defy spacetime separation.

IIIMirrors of one soul
