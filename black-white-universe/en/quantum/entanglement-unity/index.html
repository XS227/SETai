Quantum Entanglement and the Unity of Existence

Unity of Existence

SCROLL ↓

Scientific Background:
Quantum entanglement describes a phenomenon where two or more particles remain connected, even when separated by vast distances — a change in one instantly affects the other.
This non-local behavior, verified by numerous experiments (Aspect, Zeilinger, and Clauser — Nobel Prize in Physics, 2022), challenges the classical idea that information can only travel at the speed of light.
Recent experiments — like those performed at Delft University (2015) and Vienna (2017) — have confirmed “loophole-free” entanglement, showing correlations that defy spacetime separation.

Philosophical Parallel — Rumi’s Vision:
Centuries before quantum theory, Jalal al-Din Rumi wrote:

"You are not a drop in the ocean,
You are the entire ocean in a drop."

Rumi described all beings as mirrors reflecting one soul — separate in form, united in essence. His vision resonates profoundly with quantum entanglement: individuality is only apparent, while true reality is interconnected.

In the language of physics, Rumi foresaw the wavefunction of existence — a cosmic entanglement where the universe observes itself through countless particles of awareness.
