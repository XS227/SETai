CHAPTER 10 / 10

Return to the Drop

The universe within the universe. The same drop, now awake. Scroll home.

SCROLL TO READ ↓

IThe drop in the dark

In the beginning, there was only a drop —

a colorless sphere,

floating in the dark.

Within it slept a world

that had not yet been named.

And God said, “Be.”

And it was.

IIAfter all the turning

But now, after all the turning,

after light and shadow,

love and reason,

word and silence,

breath and number,

the universe returns to that same drop —

but this time, awake.

IIIA sea within a drop

We began as a drop in an infinite sea,

and now we end as a sea within a single drop.

This is the mystery of return:

Consciousness tasting itself,

light seeing itself,

God experiencing Himself — through us.

IVThe ocean returns

Rumi said:

“From the drop you came,

you became the ocean,

and in the end,

the ocean returns to the drop.”

VOpposites at rest

Science calls it Singularity —

the point where all space and time collapse into one.

Mysticism calls it Fanaa —

dissolution into truth.

And in the philosophy of Black & White,

it is the moment when opposites no longer fight,

but rest within each other.

VIThe circle never ends

The universe was a journey

from silence to sound,

from sound to light,

from light to awareness,

and from awareness, back to silence.

The circle never ends.

VIIWe only return

We never truly die;

we only return.

Each birth is a remembrance of the first,

and each death,

a doorway to beginning again.

VIIIOnly reflections

Now, within every drop,

a galaxy of light glows quietly.

And if the third eye opens,

one sees:

there are no boundaries —

only reflections.

IXThe mirror and the spark

Every human is a mirror of God,

and every God,

a spark within man.

We came from Him,

we are in Him,

and we return to Him —

not in space,

but in meaning.

XBetween two breaths

The universe is no longer outside of us.

It breathes within every breath,

speaks through every glance,

lives in every particle of love.

And now I understand:

God is not in the heavens,

nor in the future,

but here —

in the stillness between two breaths.

XIBe silent

So be silent.

Let Him create through you.

For creation has never stopped.

XIIThe conscious drop

In the beginning, there was a drop.

In the end, there is the same drop —

but now,

it is conscious.
