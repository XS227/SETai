CHAPTER 01 / 10

The Drop

SCROLL TO BEGIN ↓

In the beginning there was nothing.

No black. No white. Only a trembling possibility within the void.

We are not a drop in the ocean;

we are the ocean in a drop.

The drop takes form.

Possibility becomes motion.

We are not a drop in the ocean; we are the ocean in a drop.

Just as the word is born from breath, and light from darkness.
In the beginning there was nothing — no black, no white — only a trembling possibility within the void.
And perhaps it was there that the Creator wrote the first equation.

But the equation was incomplete, for a number without a soul is only the shadow of meaning.
Then came Love — not from outside, but from within that dark point.
And in the moment when time had not yet discovered itself, light was born from darkness.

From nonexistence comes existence.

Form rises from what had no form.

Rumi once said:

“From nonexistence comes existence, and in annihilation lies eternity.”

In today’s physics, they call it Quantum Fluctuation —
In the philosophy of Heart and Mind, it is the moment when reason begins to doubt and the heart dares to believe.
From that contradiction, being is born.

The universe is not only outside us.
We are the universe experiencing itself through the shape of a drop.
Every cell is a reflection of a galaxy;
every thought, a ripple of energy across the endless field of consciousness.

The universe is not only outside us.

The drop becomes a field of worlds.

Einstein said:

“Reality is merely an illusion, albeit a persistent one.”
Centuries before, Rumi whispered:
“This world is a dream, and only through dream can it be seen.”

Perhaps they both spoke of the same truth —
one through equations, the other through poetry.
Each knew that reality does not live in sight or in numbers alone,
but in the vibration that bridges reason and love —
the fragile edge between black and white.

Pythagoras said:

Light never truly disappears.

It changes state — and returns.

“Number is the essence of all things.”
But is a number truly devoid of feeling?
No.
Listen carefully — every number hums with its own melody:
frequency, harmony, and the silence between.
Mathematics is the language of God when He wishes to speak without words.

Rumi, too, heard this:

“The reed weeps, for it believes itself apart from the whole.”
Yet in the vision of the Black & White Universe,
we are not separate — we are the whole reflected in a single drop.
We are the Universe in a Drop,
and every drop can recreate the cosmos
once it remembers that it is the source of its own light.

In the heart of every black hole,
a spark of light waits — afraid yet unextinguished.
And within every human, that same light hides,
buried beneath logic, fear, and forgetting.
Until the heart begins to speak,
the inner black hole only knows how to swallow — not to give birth.

But when love takes the hand of reason,
time dissolves.
And then the small drop returns once more to the ocean —
not as something lost,
but as something finally awake.
