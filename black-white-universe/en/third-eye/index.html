The Third Eye

SCROLL TO READ ↓

The Return of Light

Every creation begins with sight.
But sight remains incomplete
until awareness learns to see itself through another.
We were born from the first eye —
the eye of darkness and beginning.
With the second, we came to know ourselves.
And now, through the third,
the universe awakens to its own gaze.

This third eye is not found in the forehead,
but in the soul —
where reason and love merge,
and black and white dissolve into a single light.
At that point,
the seer and the seen become one.
The universe perceives itself through the human spirit.

Rumi once said:

“Open the eye not in your head,
but the one that rests within your heart.”

Look closer.

And Christ said:

“Blessed are the pure in heart,
for they shall see God.”

To see God is to see Love within oneself.
He did not come to lift us from earth to heaven,
but to show that heaven has always been within us.
He whispered:

“The Kingdom of God is within you.”

I have learned that the third eye is the very gate to that Kingdom —
the eye that does not judge,
but forgives.

Hold the thought.

The Trinity lives in all things:
In science, the number three means balance.
In philosophy, it is the shape of completeness.
And in faith, it is the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit —
three essences revealing one God.

In this philosophy, the same pattern unfolds in consciousness:
1️⃣ Seeing
2️⃣ Knowing
3️⃣ Being

The Father — the source of light.
The Son — the reflection of that light in humanity.
The Spirit — the current of awareness flowing between them.
And in this unity,
the cosmos knows itself.

Scripture says:

Watch the idea change shape.

“Teach us to number our days,
that we may gain a heart of wisdom.” (Psalm 90:12)

What we count is not time —
but awareness.
Each number marks a stage of understanding:
1, 2, 3... then a return to 1.
The eternal rhythm — the Fibonacci of spirit.

We began as one,
found meaning in duality,
and are completed in trinity.
This is the moment of Setaei —
the Three that become One.
The Eye that is no longer seeing,
but Being Seen.

Through the third eye,
we no longer look at the universe —
we become the very act of looking.
Light returns to its source,
and darkness learns
it has always carried the seed of radiance.

In this return, everything is forgiven —
others, the past, and even the self.
Faith is no longer belief —
it is awareness.
And love is no longer emotion —
it is existence itself.
