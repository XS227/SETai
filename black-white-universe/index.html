WHAT THIS BOOK EXPLORES

Where physics ends, the questions begin.

Black & White Universe is an interactive online book by SETAEI. It moves from a drop and a point through geometry, reflection, entanglement, perception, silence and language, using visual storytelling to make difficult questions memorable.

The research section connects the journey to established quantum experiments and concepts: the double-slit experiment, delayed choice, Bell inequalities, tunneling, teleportation, superposition and entanglement. The open-question section separates experimental results from interpretation, metaphor and speculation.

Read in English, Norwegian or Persian. You can also jump directly to quantum experiments or the open questions.
