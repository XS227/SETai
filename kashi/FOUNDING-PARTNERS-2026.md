# KASHI – Founding Partners 2026
Date: 2026-11-27 | Agent handoff | Status: recruitment plan / unverified prospect seed

## Owner-approved business direction
- KASHI.IS v2 is a multi-vendor marketplace connecting buyers with sellers and earning revenue on orders attributed to KASHI. Buyers use the portal free of charge.
- Standard annual seller fee: NOK 12,000. Sellers joining by 31 December 2026 receive 50% discount (NOK 6,000 annual introductory fee).
- Start active advertising and SEO from 2027. Recruit initial sellers before then, beginning with businesses like Stenhuset. Start in Norway and Iceland; support sellers by country and city internationally.
- Sellers retain their own brands, catalogues and prices. AI search should guide buyers to suitable products and sellers.
- Proposed (not yet approved): retain 50% discount on uninterrupted renewals; 30 founding partners before 2027; commission rate and base, renewal/eligibility mechanics, VAT, billing dates, payment/refunds, currencies and contract terms require owner approval. Do not assume commission is 5%; that was an illustrative scenario only.
