# KASHI Marketplace V2 — engineering handoff
Status: review branch, 2026-11-27. Do not merge or deploy without owner approval. Target path /v2/ on Kashi.is; preserve existing root site and 3D assets. This is a static review with synthetic listings, local storage and no genuine AI, real payment or shared database. Do not collect real personal or business information in this version.

## Product direction
International multi-vendor marketplace for tiles, stone, architectural surfaces and related services. Start with Iceland, support arbitrary country/city combinations without implying that all trade corridors are permitted. Buyer, seller and authenticated admin roles; commission per eligible completed transaction. Search is the front door, not merely a chat widget.

## Review implementation
- kashi/v2/index.html: existing design, sample catalog, cart, quote, commission ledger, seller/admin workspaces and design studio.
- Global application: ISO country/territory codes, free-form city, postal code, company registration, business email, website, product categories, currency, delivery regions. Local pending application directory with country/city filtering. No actual seller verification or publication.
- Smart discovery: natural-language keyword/material and light/dark matching over sample listings, explicit area/budget/destination fields, estimated material subtotal, studio/quote navigation. This is a deterministic search prototype, not an LLM. Destination availability and suitability are not verified.
- Languages: EN/IS/FA in original UI are partial; new components initially English. Rule matching includes selected Norwegian, Icelandic and Persian terms.

## P0 — persistence, identity and global seller network
Create migrations for countries, cities (country-specific identifiers, normalized names and aliases), sellers, seller_locations, seller_users, applications, seller_delivery_regions, products, seller_offers, inventory, product_certifications, media, audit_events. Use UUIDs, ISO currency/country codes, timezone, locale and integer minor units; never infer country from a city name alone. Search location by country/city and delivery coverage, with geocoding only when consent/terms allow.
Support seller types: manufacturer, distributor, retailer, installer and logistics provider. Admin can create prospect records from publicly available business info, invite sellers with permission, de-duplicate by registration number and country; record consent, provenance and status (prospect/invited/pending/verified/suspended). Only verified approved seller offers appear as purchasable. Self-registration, catalogue CSV/API import with validation, manual review, permissions and tenant isolation. Do not scrape gated catalogues or imply partnership before agreement.

## P0 — KASHI AI sales and discovery
Implement server-side POST /api/v2/search and /api/v2/assistant/message; structured request: locale, destination, session ID, project type, room, area, budget with currency, material, finish, color, size, constraints. Return product/offer IDs, facts and source timestamps, clarifying questions, reason for match, available actions, exclusions. No secrets in browser. Use hybrid lexical + semantic retrieval with deterministic filters for active offers, delivery eligibility, region, stock and certification. Use current catalog, seller coverage, inventory, price, shipping quotes and policy APIs as tools; model output cannot set prices, choose payout details or fabricate facts. No results -> ask useful follow-up, offer quote, or escalate; never invent stock, safety classification or ETA. For wet floors/exteriors/frost, require verified technical documentation.
Interaction: free text in hero, multilingual search, suggestions, optional image inspiration (later, consent and privacy), compare products, calculate area/quantity with packaging and waste allowance explicitly disclosed, open existing 3D studio, quote/cart, delivery estimate, checkout. Preserve selected seller/offer and session attribution. Revalidate price and eligibility on cart and checkout. User explicitly confirms checkout, quotes and seller contact; no autonomous purchases. Display paid placement transparently and rank organic relevance ahead of commission. Provide human escalation. Track anonymous search-to-product, quote, cart, completed order conversion and attributed commission; consent and retention policies.
AI provider abstraction, rate limits, evaluation sets (EN/IS/NO/FA), prompt-injection defense for catalog text, structured schema validation, secrets server-side, circuit breaker and deterministic fallback. No provider selected yet.

## P0 — revenue, checkout, order lifecycle
Server-owned commission rules: seller/offer-specific effective-dated basis points, snapshot on order line, calculate integer minor units using explicit rounding; define taxable amounts and agreed treatment of discount, VAT, shipping, return and payment fees. Proposed pilot default configurable, not a contract. Split multi-seller basket into parent order and seller orders; record buyer payment, fulfillment, refunds and commission in append-only ledger. Idempotent verified payment webhooks, reconciliation, payout only through authorized marketplace PSP after fulfillment/return conditions. Quote workflow: accepted quote creates platform order linked to seller, commission and originating session. Do not claim revenue on unpaid or refunded orders. Separate gross merchandise value, recognized commission, payout, taxes and costs. No off-platform payment instructions or bypass.
Seller/importer of record, consumer rights, VAT/customs, product safety, data protection and provider/country eligibility must be reviewed before real sales. Sanctions and restricted-party screening, permitted corridors, product checks and payment-provider approval are launch gates; deny transactions where eligibility is unresolved. No methods to evade restrictions.

## P1 — experience and scale
Country/city landing pages using real verified inventory; canonical/hreflang, structured Product/Offer data only where eligible, no fake pages. Product photos with permission, accessible UI, fully localized EN/IS/NO/FA + RTL, mobile and currency handling. Preserve the existing 3D showroom or integrate a verified 3D module; prototype is only 2D. Seller CSV import, invitations, bulk review, reports, local sourcing and shipping integrations. Admin map by city using aggregated non-sensitive counts. Seller support and notification preferences.
