Our story / KASHI Iceland

Materials withmeaning.

KASHI is a developing Icelandic tile showroom concept. Our ambition is to connect Nordic interiors with carefully selected porcelain and ceramic surfaces sourced from Iranian manufacturers.

Made for Iceland

We focus on practical formats, transparent project pricing and material choices suitable for homes and professional projects. Technical suitability for wet or outdoor applications must be verified per product.

Transparent sourcing

We intend to disclose product origin, manufacturer information, specifications, availability and confirmed lead times. KASHI is not yet an established trading company and products are not yet available to purchase.
