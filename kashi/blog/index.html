Porcelain vs ceramic: what is the difference?

Porcelain typically has lower water absorption than ordinary ceramic wall tiles. Selection should be based on intended use, verified specifications, slip resistance and installation requirements.

How many square metres should you order?

Measure length × width, then add an allowance for cuts and breakage. Straightforward layouts may need less allowance than diagonal or intricate patterns. The price studio rounds to whole boxes.

Choosing tiles for Icelandic conditions

Outdoor and wet-area suitability must be documented by the individual manufacturer's test reports. Frost resistance, surface grip and substrate design are important; a visual appearance alone is not enough.
