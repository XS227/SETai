04 / Go-to-market plan

12 months. Three stages.

Months 1–3 · Validation

Confirm sanctions compliance, bank acceptance, shipping, certificates, supplier SKUs and sample quality. Launch Icelandic/English catalog and quote flow. Collect B2B letters of intent.

Months 4–6 · First orders

Small pilot inventory, sample kits, contractor outreach and localized landing pages. Track quote-to-order rate, landed costs and average basket size.

Months 7–9 · Acquisition

Publish installation guides, bathroom inspiration, local delivery FAQs and case studies. Test Google Ads only after supply and fulfillment are proven.

Months 10–12 · Scale decision

Compare product-level contribution, reorder frequency, inventory turnover and cash conversion before committing to a larger shipment.

06 / The Google playbook

How KASHI wins
on Google.

A brand-new domain section, no stock yet and strong incumbents. The plan is to avoid the head-on fight, win the searches where a visual, price-transparent tile supplier is genuinely more useful, and only push for volume once supply is proven.

Thesis 1

Skip the head terms. Own the tools.

“flísar” is held by Bauhaus, Húsasmiðjan, Flísabúðin, Álfaborg and Egill Árnason, all with years of authority. Year one is about long-tail intent where a calculator, a 3D room and visible prices answer the question better: how many m², price per m² 60×60, tiles for bathroom + heated floor, tiles for contractors.

Thesis 2

Real Icelandic URLs, not a toggle.

The EN/IS/FA switch on the pages is client-side. It is great for visitors but Google only indexes the English source. To rank in Icelandic, every priority page needs its own server-rendered URL under /kashi/is/ with hreflang and native-reviewed copy.

Thesis 3

Compliance gates the launch.

Iceland aligned with the EU's updated Iran restrictive measures in July 2026. Whether tile import and the payment route are permitted must be confirmed by customs, the bank and counsel before we invest in indexing, ads or stock. SEO work in Phase 0–1 is reversible; inventory is not.

Months 4–9 · Commerce & paid

  • Add English and Persian versions only for pages that proved demand (expats, diaspora, trade).

Biggest risks to this plan

Sanctions and payment

If the route is not cleared, there is nothing to rank for. Keep Phase 1 work reversible and delay Ads and stock.

Thin or over-promising pages

Pre-launch pages that imply availability can hurt trust and breach consumer rules. Keep “candidates, not stock” wording until it is true.

Translation quality

Machine-translated Icelandic ranks and converts poorly. Native review is a hard requirement before an /is/ page goes live.

Authority gap

New domain section, no backlinks. Expect months, not weeks. Link-worthy tools and real project proof are the shortcut.
